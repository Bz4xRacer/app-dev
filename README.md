## My Favorite Series & Movies

### 🏎️ **Initial D**
- **Genre**: Racing, Action, Sports
- **About**: A teenage delivery boy becomes an amazing downhill racing legend using his father's Toyota AE86
- **Why I like it**: Incredible street racing scenes and iconic Eurobeat soundtrack

### 🏁 **Ford v Ferrari**
- **Genre**: Drama, Action, Biography  
- **About**: American car designer Carroll Shelby and driver Ken Miles battle corporate interference to build a revolutionary race car for Ford
- **Why I like it**: Amazing racing sequences and great performances

### 🕶️ **Kingsman**
- **Genre**: Action, Adventure, Comedy
- **About**: A secret spy organization recruits a street kid into their training program
- **Why I like it**: Stylish action scenes and clever humor

### 🚗 **Transporter**
- **Genre**: Action, Crime, Thriller
- **About**: A professional getaway driver will transport anything for the right price
- **Why I like it**: Intense car chases and great fight scenes

### 👼 **Legion**
- **Genre**: Action, Drama, Fantasy
- **About**: A troubled young man discovers he has incredible powers in a world where mutants exist
